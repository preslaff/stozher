# STOZHER terms of use for admitted organisations — DRAFT v0.1

> Draft for discussion. Not legal advice; it needs legal review before any organisation signs it.
> The platform records the version an organisation accepted (`agreement_version`); the signed copy is kept outside the platform.

STOZHER provides situational awareness and evidence about GNSS reliability and, where enabled, community
hazard reports on roads and places. It supports civil protection, rescue, infrastructure and public-safety
work. Access is restricted to admitted organisations.

1. **Purpose.** Use the platform and its data only for the purposes stated when each credential was issued.
2. **Credentials.** Every credential belongs to your organisation and has a stated purpose and an expiry.
   Keep secrets out of source code and shared channels. Staff use named accounts; never share a staff credential.
   Report a lost or exposed credential at once to the platform administrators.
3. **People.** The platform is about places, not people. Do not use it to identify, follow or profile an
   individual, and do not try to infer who made a report.
4. **Reports.** Submit only what you observed. An unverified report is shown as unverified; do not present it as confirmed.
5. **Data you receive.** Redistribute only as the platform's role and licence terms allow. Treat simulated
   data as simulated.
6. **Contact.** Keep a responsible contact up to date. We may review your access periodically.
7. **Suspension.** We may suspend an organisation and revoke all its credentials immediately where abuse is
   suspected or a credential may be exposed. Credentials end when the organisation leaves.
8. **No warranty.** Information is provided as evidence with stated confidence and age. Decisions remain yours.
